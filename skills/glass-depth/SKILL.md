## Operation and feature contract

`scene.author@1`, `scene.render@1`, `scene.frame@1`, `inspection.visual@1`; require exact compositing/refraction controls in the selected schema. Requested transparent imagery/video needs `image.alpha`/`video.alpha` respectively plus actual decoding/probe evidence.

## Role procedure

1. Distinguish true supported refraction, backdrop filtering and a translucent approximation. Bind only advertised effects and obtain a changed treatment decision before substituting a cheaper visual illusion.

2. Define layer depth, edge highlight, shadow, blur and text planes with explicit compositing order. Keep type outside distortion where possible and specify straight versus premultiplied alpha consistently.

3. Render the card against both bright and dark backgrounds, including the least favorable text region. Adjust surface opacity or add a readable backing when transparency causes content to disappear.

4. For requested alpha, inspect decoded alpha-plane evidence and composites over contrasting backgrounds. A PNG extension or apparently transparent checkerboard is not proof; no silent flattening to make a renderer succeed.

## Outputs and evidence

- Layer/material source, glass scene and contrast/alpha proofs.

Return material/layer source, actual contrast frames, approximations and alpha proofs when requested. Unsupported refraction or alpha remains a named gap, not a falsely completed glass effect.

## Acceptance SK-S32-output

A glass card is readable on bright and dark backgrounds; unsupported refraction is disclosed, and requested alpha requires actual alpha-plane proof rather than a flattened image.
