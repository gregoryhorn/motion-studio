# Continuous Scene

## Trigger and exclusions

Author a continuous no-cut scene. Exclude hiding cuts as a substitute when continuity is a requirement.

## Required inputs

- Spatial subjects, narrative path, duration, camera/layout constraints.

## Operation and feature contract

`scene.author@1`, `scene.render@1`, `scene.frame@1`, `inspection.visual@1`, `inspection.playback@1`; require the selected `scene.2d` or `scene.3d` plus `timing.frame_seek`. Camera controls must exist in the inspected source schema.

## Role procedure

1. Draw a spatial route through the subjects with stable object identities and a common coordinate system. Establish where the viewer starts, what remains visible during travel and where each readable pause occurs.

2. Allocate travel and holds inside the approved frame range. Make camera position, framing or layout progression a seekable function of frame; do not reset object transforms between subjects or hide a scene replacement behind a wipe.

3. Check intermediate occlusion, cropping and travel speed, not just composition at the three stops. For a 2D route, keep layer order continuous; for 3D, check near-plane intersections and unintended camera clipping.

4. Render every handoff interval and the full passage. Inspect identity continuity, velocity changes and dwell readability in playback. A boundary jump fails even if the endpoints are attractive; revise only the offending route interval.

## Outputs and evidence

- Continuous scene source, camera/transition plan and continuity evidence.

The camera/transition plan records frame intervals, subject IDs, spatial anchors and holds. Continuity findings cite neighbor frames around each handoff and actual full-playback coverage; an occluded hidden cut is not an accepted solution.

## Acceptance SK-S10-output

A three-subject traverse has no discontinuous cut, preserves object identity and offers readable pauses; inspect every handoff and full playback, not just endpoints.
