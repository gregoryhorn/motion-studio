# Cut Paper Treatment

## Trigger and exclusions

Create an original layered cut-paper treatment. Exclude copying a protected illustration or mandatory texture download.

## Required inputs

- Approved motif, supplied textures or authored palette, depth and timing.

## Operation and feature contract

`scene.author@1`, `scene.render@1`, `scene.frame@1`, `inspection.visual@1`; selected source schema must support layers/compositing. Require `image.alpha` or `video.alpha` only when transparent output is requested, and `timing.frame_seek` for edge samples.

## Role procedure

1. Confirm approved motif, depth and timing and record whether textures are licensed, user-supplied or authored. Do not trace protected illustration or download a texture pack as a setup step.

2. Split the landscape into intentional paper silhouettes with stable stacking and editable contours. Define thickness cues, shadow direction and shadow ownership so foreground motion cannot leave detached shadows.

3. Choose bounded parallax by depth and provide overscan beyond the final crop. Check moving layer coverage: paper edges may be irregular by design but cannot tear open unintended holes or reveal missing background.

4. Render maximum travel and overlap extremes plus neighboring frames. Inspect alpha edges, occlusion order, shadow direction and texture continuity. Missing texture only permits an explicitly accepted authored alternative under a revised treatment.

## Outputs and evidence

- Editable layers, scene source/render and edge/occlusion evidence.

Return editable layers, scene source/render and frame-indexed edge/occlusion findings. A three-layer fixture must preserve intended shadow order during motion, not only at the initial still.

A three-layer landscape retains clean edges during parallax and intended shadow order; missing texture uses only an explicitly accepted authored alternative.
