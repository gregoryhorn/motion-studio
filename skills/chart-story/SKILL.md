# Chart Story

## Trigger and exclusions

Animate supplied numeric data into an explanatory chart. Exclude unsupported estimates and general infographic art.

## Required inputs

- Dataset with units/source dates, intended comparison, brief and frame range.

## Operation and feature contract

`scene.author@1`, `scene.render@1`, `scene.frame@1`, `inspection.visual@1`; select `scene.vector` or `scene.raster` explicitly and require `timing.frame_seek` for samples. Dataset reading and validation are core work.

## Role procedure

1. Build a row ledger retaining original values, units, category labels, source locator and observation dates. Distinguish missing from zero, verify sums only where a total is meaningful, and stop numeric claims when units or the intended comparison are unresolved.

2. Choose an encoding appropriate to the question: bars for magnitude, lines only for an ordered domain. Declare a common scale and axis origin; reject a cropped bar baseline that exaggerates difference. Calculate geometry from the retained data, not hand-positioned labels.

3. Separate data state from presentation state. Animate a reveal mask, annotation or focus stroke rather than changing the labelled observation to fit a dramatic peak. Keep a truthful final value visible while interpolated geometry is explicitly a transition.

4. Lay out longest labels and source notes before adding decoration. Sample the first readable state, peak emphasis and final hold at delivery dimensions; compare every displayed number and axis tick back to the row ledger.

## Outputs and evidence

- Data-linked chart source, scene manifest and labelled preview frames.

Retain a field-to-geometry/label mapping and scale specification beside editable chart source; frame evidence records which source rows were checked. Do not use a generated chart image as numerical evidence.

## Acceptance SK-S01-output

With 10, 20, 15 labelled units, inspect start/peak/end frames: values and axis origin remain correct, labels readable, provenance retained; missing units stops numeric assertions.
